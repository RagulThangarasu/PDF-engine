Each chapter runs from its top-level heading to the next one, on both sides at once, however many pages that takes in each document — the two paginate differently, so pages are never compared by number. A paragraph that wraps onto another line, or carries over onto the next page, is put back together before anything is compared, so neither is reported as a difference. Running headers, footers, page numbers and printed “see page N” cross-references are left out for the same reason. Tables of contents and Q&A / FAQ sections are not compared at all — neither as chapters nor inside one — since they are page-number listings, and the embedded outline has its own TOC comparison.
